Methodology & California Prop 13 Context

1. Uncapped Formula (B25090 ÷ B01003)

Census table B25103 (Median Real Estate Taxes Paid) is top-coded at $10,001 (“$10,000+”) across most Bay Area ZIP codes, masking upper-tier price and tax dynamics. Instead, this tracker divides uncapped aggregate residential real estate taxes paid (B25090_E001) by total resident population (B01003_E001), and also computes uncapped mean tax per owner-occupied unit (B25090_E001 ÷ B25003_E002).

2. Prop 13 & Quality-of-Living Signal

Under California Proposition 13, assessed property values for continuing owners rise at most 2% per year until a sale or new construction resets assessment to market value. Consequently, ZIPs where per-capita residential tax rises materially faster than 2% annually reflect active buyer turnover, housing upgrades, and higher resident-funded local tax capacity. Watch the Tax Pool vs. Pop decomposition to verify gains come from tax-base growth rather than population decline.

3. 2019 ACS Redesign & Data Scope

Metrics cover owner-occupied residential housing units reported in U.S. Census ACS 5-Year ZCTA estimates (excluding commercial/industrial parcels and ZCTAs under 100 residents). In 2019, the Census redesigned the tax question from intervals to exact dollar write-ins, phasing fully into 5-year estimates over 2019–2023. Use the 2023 → 2024 or 2021 → 2024 horizon for pure post-redesign momentum, and re-run scripts/fetch_bay_area_tax_data.py when new ACS vintages release.
